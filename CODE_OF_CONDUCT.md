# Code of Conduct

DriveVault follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree politely and explain why.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual content, threats or publishing other people's private information.
- Help keep users safe: don't post anyone's vault data, recovery keys or real file names.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@svrsunny](https://github.com/svrsunny)) may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem without posting it publicly, use GitHub's
"Report content" option on the comment, or GitHub's reporting for abuse.
