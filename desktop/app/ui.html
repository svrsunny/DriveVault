Choose the drive to protect
DriveVault creates an encrypted DriveVault folder on the drive you pick and copies itself there, so you can open it from that drive on any computer. Other files on the drive are not changed.

  
  

  




Your recovery key

  
If the password is ever forgotten, this key resets it. Store it somewhere safe and away from the drive: printed, in a password manager, or in a photo on your phone.

Security

  
Password
Used to unlock the vault.

  
Recovery key 
A 25-character key that resets the password. The strongest recovery option.

  
Security questions 
Three questions only you can answer.

  
Trusted Android phone 
Reset a forgotten password from your phone with its fingerprint / PIN. Set it up in the DriveVault Android app: Security → Trust this phone.

  





Security questions

  
Questions are weaker than a recovery key: anyone who has the drive and can guess or find all three answers (social media, friends, family) can reset the password. Pick answers only you know. Spelling, capitals and punctuation don't matter.
